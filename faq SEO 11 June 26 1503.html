DISC + EQ Assessments

What is the difference between DISC and EQ?

DISC profiles observable external behaviours — how you act, communicate, and respond under pressure. It identifies your dominant style across four dimensions: Dominance, Influence, Steadiness, and Conscientiousness.

EQ (Emotional Intelligence) measures internal skills — how well you recognise, understand, and regulate emotions in yourself and others.

Used together, they create the most complete diagnostic picture for leadership development, team performance, and conflict resolution in Singapore SME environments.

What is the Free DISC Leadership Snapshot tool?

The Free DISC Leadership Snapshot is a complimentary online assessment that takes approximately 5 minutes to complete. It analyses your dominant DISC behavioural profile and generates an instant results dashboard including radar and bar charts, career role suggestions, leadership insights, and dual-dominant profile detection. Results can be downloaded or printed as a PDF.

Does the Free DISC tool store my personal data?

No. The Free DISC Leadership Snapshot is entirely client-side — no personal data is transmitted to or stored on any server. Your results are generated and displayed in your browser only, fully compliant with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). You can save your results by downloading the PDF.

How is a full DISC + EQ assessment different from the free tool?

The free tool is a concise leadership snapshot — ideal for personal awareness and exploration. A full certified DISC + EQ assessment includes a deeper diagnostic report, a debrief session with a certified practitioner, team-level analysis, and an action plan tailored to your leadership development goals. Contact us to learn more about the full assessment.
