About TalentEdge Asia

A Singapore-based advisory firm built to help SMEs and PMEs perform at their best — through executive coaching, enterprise training, behavioural diagnostics, and digital innovation.

Who We Are

TalentEdge Asia operates under Talentedge Performance LLP, a licensed advisory practice registered in Singapore. Founded in 2025, we were built on a single conviction: that sustainable performance — for individuals and organisations alike — comes from developing people, not just processes.

We serve Singapore's SME owners, senior leaders, and PMEs across industries — providing the frameworks, tools, and coaching support needed to navigate career transitions, build resilient teams, and embrace digital transformation with confidence.

Our practice integrates four complementary disciplines: ICF-aligned executive coaching, SkillsFuture enterprise training, DISC and EQ diagnostics, and digital innovation advisory — all under one roof, with a team of certified practitioners who have led in the fields they advise on.
