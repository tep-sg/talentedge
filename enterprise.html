The SkillsFuture SFw Strategic Advantage for Singapore SMEs

Our foundation is the Skills Framework (SFw), developed by SkillsFuture Singapore (SSG) to provide industry-standard benchmarks for workforce excellence. We translate these national standards into practical corporate training programmes your team can apply immediately.
